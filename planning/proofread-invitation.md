# Invitation copy — proof-reading sheet

Every guest-visible line on **tomyjeyan.com**, pulled from the live markup on
**July 31, 2026** in reading order. This is a working file in `planning/` — it is
not part of the website and never deploys.

How to use it: read top to bottom, mark anything wrong, and send me the list. I
change it in the page, not in this file.

Three notes before you start:

- The **countdown numbers** ("149 DAYS…") are live and change every second. Ignore them.
- The **RSVP panel** shows every possible state at once here (the "reply-by date has
  passed" line, "Change my response", "Not you? Start fresh"). A guest only ever
  sees the ones that apply to them.
- **Names are the highest-risk item.** The Entourage section is worth reading twice,
  ideally against Jeyan's own list — spelling, titles, and who is paired with whom.

## FAQ & Contact

```
Q: Can I bring a plus one?
A: Our guest list is by name, so the seats reserved for you are shown when you
   RSVP. If you're unsure how many seats are yours, message us and we'll gladly
   confirm.

Q: What should I wear?
A: Smart formal in soft, dreamy tones: cream, ivory, champagne, sage, dusty rose,
   soft taupe. See the Attire Guide for the full palette. Kindly skip loud
   colours, heavy black, and neon.

Q: Where can I park?
A: On-site parking is available at both St. Therese Parish (Newport City, Pasay)
   and Gallio Events Hall (Aseana City, Parañaque). The two are about a 15-20
   minute drive apart, so ride-hailing is easy too.

Q: What time should I arrive?
A: The ceremony begins at 1:30 PM sharp. We suggest being seated by 1:00 PM so
   you can settle in before the doors close for the processional.

Q: Are children invited?
A: We love your little ones. Seats are limited, so please include everyone
   joining you by name in your RSVP so we can plan their places with care.

STILL NEED US? THE HELP DESK
Questions about seats, directions, or the day itself? Our crew is happy to help.
```
