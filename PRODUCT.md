# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Invited guests of Tommy & Jeyan's wedding — family, close friends, principal and secondary sponsors, and the entourage, primarily in the Philippines. They arrive via a privately shared access code, most often on a phone, sometimes weeks before the day and sometimes the morning of. Their job is simple and emotional: understand the essentials (date, ceremony and reception venues, attire, how to RSVP), get a feel for the couple's story, and reserve their seat. Many are not tech-savvy, so nothing should require explanation.

## Product Purpose

A private digital wedding invitation for **Tommy & Jeyan**, Sunday December 27, 2026 (ceremony 1:30 PM at St. Therese Parish; reception at Savoy Hotel Manila, Newport City, Pasay). It exists to (1) deliver the practical details clearly, (2) immerse guests in the couple's **"Soaring with TJ — The Journey"** travel narrative, and (3) collect RSVPs through a native form backed by a Google Apps Script endpoint. Success = a guest feels personally invited and delighted, finds what they need in seconds, and RSVPs without friction. The couple were civilly married in 2024 (a program reveal) — the access code `dec272024` is an intentional nod, not a typo.

## Brand Personality

Dreamy, airy, soaring. The voice is warm, personal, and quietly playful — it borrows travel and flight metaphors (boarding passes, itineraries, "our next chapter") with a light touch, never as a costume. It should feel hand-written for these two people: tender, celebratory, and calm. Emotional goals: anticipation, intimacy, and unhurried joy. Premium without being showy; romantic without being saccharine.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI/wedding-template look** — the previous cream "editorial" build (Cormorant Garamond + Inter, big serif hero, eyebrow small-caps) that reads as a default harness output. Explicitly the thing we are moving away from.
- **Dark luxe** (deep navy + gold + heavy calligraphy, e.g. the Ace & Apple reference) — the opposite of the TOR's light, airy mandate. Useful only as interaction inspiration, never as palette.
- **Overcrowded / overly colorful / floral-maximalist** layouts (the TOR explicitly warns against this).
- **Clip-art infographics** and stocky icon-people (the old flat dress-code graphic).
- Anything loud, corporate, or that buries the couple's own photos under chrome.

## Design Principles

- **The journey is the throughline.** Every section is a beat in a travel story (flight-path dividers, boarding-pass cards, "our next chapter") — cohesive narrative, never a gimmick. If a travel motif doesn't earn its place, cut it.
- **Airy over ornate.** Whitespace, light, and restraint carry the elegance, per the TOR. When in doubt, remove rather than add.
- **The couple's photos are the heart.** Real prenup imagery leads; type and ornament support it. Curate ruthlessly to couple-only frames.
- **Personal, not templated.** Copy and details should feel written by hand for Tommy & Jeyan, not assembled from a kit.
- **Effortless for guests.** Date, venues, attire, and RSVP are findable in seconds on a phone, by someone who isn't tech-savvy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its (often tinted) background — guard against muted gray on cream. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (the page is animation-rich: intro reveal, scroll reveals, a scroll-driven plane). Keyboard-accessible navigation, lightbox, and RSVP form; visible focus states; descriptive `alt` text on every photo; legible type sizes on small screens. No information conveyed by color alone.
