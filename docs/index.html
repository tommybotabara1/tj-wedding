FAQ & Contact

Can I bring a plus one?

Our guest list is by name, so the seats reserved for you are shown when you RSVP. If you're unsure how many seats are yours, message us and we'll gladly confirm.

What should I wear?

Smart formal in soft, dreamy tones: cream, ivory, champagne, sage, dusty rose, soft taupe. See the Attire Guide for the full palette. Kindly skip loud colours, heavy black, and neon.

Where can I park?

On-site parking is available at both St. Therese Parish (Newport City, Pasay) and Gallio Events Hall (Aseana City, Parañaque). The two are about a 15–20 minute drive apart, so ride-hailing is easy too.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins at 1:30 PM sharp. We suggest being seated by 1:00 PM so you can settle in before the doors close for the processional.

Are children invited?

We love your little ones. Seats are limited, so please include everyone joining you by name in your RSVP so we can plan their places with care.

Still need us? The help desk

Questions about seats, directions, or the day itself? Our crew is happy to help.
